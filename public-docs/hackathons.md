# Embarko for Hackathons

Give every participant **2 months of Embarko Pro — free**, and get a
dedicated Showcase page for your event.

Example: `embarko.ai/showcase/iit-indore-2026`

Participants deploy their projects on Embarko, submit them to Showcase, and
your event gets one permanent page where judges, participants, companies and
the community can browse what was built.

## [What you get](#what-you-get)

- **2 months of Embarko Pro for every participant**
- **A unique hackathon code**
- **A dedicated Showcase page for your event**
- One place for judges to browse live projects
- A permanent gallery after the event
- Simple deployment from participants' AI agents
- No hosting setup or hosting workshop required

## [How it works](#how-it-works)

### 1. Apply

Send us:

- Event name
- College / organisation
- Event dates
- Expected number of participants
- Event website or social link

### 2. We create your event

We generate a **hackathon code**:

```
IITINDORE26
```

and a dedicated Showcase page:

```
embarko.ai/showcase/iit-indore-2026
```

### 3. Share the code with participants

Participants use the code to activate **2 months of Embarko Pro — free**.

### 4. Participants build and deploy

They build with the AI agent they already use, then ask it:

```
Deploy this to Embarko
```

Their project gets a live URL. See [`docs.md`](docs.md) for what actually
happens on a deploy, and [`troubleshoot.md`](troubleshoot.md) if a build
fails.

### 5. Projects join your Showcase

Participants submit their live app to Embarko Showcase and select your
hackathon. Their project appears on your event page, so judges and your
community have one place to browse everything built during the event.

## [Keep Embarko Pro free for 1 year](#keep-embarko-pro-free-for-1-year)

Participants can keep Embarko Pro free for a year by showing what they built.

Post a short **Instagram Reel or YouTube video** showing:

1. What you built
2. Who it helps
3. A quick demo
4. How you put it online with Embarko

Tag Embarko and submit the video link. If approved: **1 year of Embarko Pro
— free**.

Good projects may also be featured in Embarko Showcase and shared with the
Embarko community.

## [Your hackathon lives beyond the event](#your-hackathon-lives-beyond-the-event)

Your Showcase page stays online after the hackathon. Use it to:

- show winning projects
- share shortlisted projects
- let judges browse live submissions
- showcase your participants
- share the event with companies and communities
- keep projects discoverable after the hackathon
